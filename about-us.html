ABOUT DE SQUARE GLOBAL

Bridging the Gap Between Academia and Industry

We are a forward-thinking education consultancy dedicated to bridging the gap between academia and industry.

At our core, a commitment to employability drives us to design performance-based curricula that mirror real-world job market demands.

We empower educators through targeted faculty training, transforming them into facilitators who deliver impactful, industry-aligned education for the future of work.

Company Owners

This partnership combines WIDS‘ global expertise in performance-based curriculum design with De Square Global’s deep understanding of regional needs,
serving as the exclusive agent and distributor for curriculum management software and consulting solutions across Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Bahrain, Oman, Qatar, Iraq, Lebanon, and Egypt. De Square Global’s qualified team delivers comprehensive WIDS training and technical support — from initial setup to advanced program design workshops — with multi-lingual local support that upholds WIDS‘ rigorous global standards.
