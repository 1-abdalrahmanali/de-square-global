WIDS Clients in the Middle East (G6 Countries)

Colleges of Excellence (COE) – Saudi Arabia

On behalf of the Technical and Vocational Training Corporation (TVTC) in Saudi Arabia, COE contracted with WIDS and DeSquare Global to conduct 15 DACUM Quality Benchmark Reviews. In addition, WIDS and De Square Global conducted in depth Benchmark and Quality Review processes for 15 programs based on best practices in the U.S.

Saudi Ministry of Education

The Saudi Ministry of Education, the Human Capability Development Program, and the Applied Colleges Initiative collaborated with WIDS and De Square Global to develop 11 programs utilizing the DACUM process, WIDS software, and methodology. In addition, WIDS and De Square Global conducted in-depth Benchmark and Quality Review processes for these 11 programs based on best practices in the U.S.

ARRC Consulting – Saudi Arabia

On behalf of TVTC, ARRC Consulting contracted with WIDS and De Square Global to conduct more than 50 DACUM and Program Quality review processes. WIDS, De Square Global, and ARRC continue to refine the Quality Review processes to better meet the needs of TVTC.

United Arab Emirates Ministry of Education

The Ministry of Education contracted with WIDS/ De Square Global for five years to develop and manage their national curriculum framework for K-12 public schools and Technical vocational schools.
