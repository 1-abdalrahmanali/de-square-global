WIDS DACUM Facilitator Certificate

Developing Expert DACUM Facilitators for Industry-Validated Curriculum

About the DACUM Method

DACUM — Developing A CurriculUM — is a proven occupational analysis methodology used worldwide to identify the duties, tasks, knowledge, and skills required for success in a specific occupation. Developed through structured expert panel sessions, DACUM results serve as the foundation for designing rigorous, industry-validated, performance-based curricula. WIDS has been a leader in DACUM facilitation and training for over three decades, supporting technical colleges, government agencies, and industry partners across the United States and internationally.

Workshop Overview

This intensive 5-day certification workshop equips instructional designers, curriculum developers, and education professionals with the complete skillset needed to plan, facilitate, and implement DACUM studies. Participants engage in hands-on activities, guided practice, and peer collaboration throughout the training.

Format Options

  • In-Person (on-site)
  • Virtual (live online via video conferencing)
  • Hybrid delivery available upon request

Training Details

  • Duration: 5 Full Days
  • Cohort Size: Up to 20 Participants
  • Language: English/Arabic

WIDS DACUM Facilitator certificate

Participants who successfully meet performance criteria will receive the WIDS DACUM Facilitator Certificate, demonstrating their readiness to plan, facilitate, and apply DACUM occupational analysis in professional settings.

Certification is based on demonstrated performance assessed using the WIDS DACUM Certification Rubric, completed on Day 5 of the workshop. Participants will have opportunities throughout the week to practice each competency and receive expert feedback before the final assessment.

5-Day Workshop Schedule

Day 01

Plan a DACUM Occupational Analysis Project Develop DACUM Components

Day 02

Facilitate the DACUM Process Conduct a Modified or Virtual DACUM

Day 03

Create a DACUM Chart, Validation Survey, and Follow-up Communication Plan

Day 04

Relate DACUM Results to the Development of a Performance-Based Instructional Design

Day 05

DACUM Certification Assessment

  1. Plan a DACUM occupational analysis project from initial scoping through logistics and panel recruitment
  2. Develop DACUM components including duties, tasks, and supporting knowledge and skills
  3. Facilitate the DACUM process effectively, guiding expert panels to produce accurate, validated occupational data
  4. Conduct a modified or virtual DACUM using alternative methods and digital facilitation tools
  5. Create a DACUM chart, validation survey, and follow-up communication plan
  6. Relate DACUM results to the development of a performance-based instructional design

Who Should Attend

This training is designed for professionals involved in workforce development, curriculum design, and technical education, including:

  • Curriculum developers and instructional designers
  • Technical college faculty and academic administrators

About WIDS

The Worldwide Instructional Design System (WIDS) is a division of the Wisconsin Technical College System Foundation (WTCSF), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Since 1993, WIDS has provided curriculum design tools, consulting, and professional development to educational institutions, government agencies, and industry partners across the United States, Canada, and the Middle East. With decades of experience in instructional design, competency-based learning, and program development, WIDS consultants help organizations create practical, high-quality learning solutions that improve educational and workforce outcomes.
