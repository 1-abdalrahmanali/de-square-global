About De Square Global

Bridging Education & Industry

De Square Global helps educational institutions build practical, performance-based curriculum aligned with real job market needs. We deliver this through two complementary WIDS certified workshops: the WIDS DACUM Facilitator Workshop trains participants to plan and lead DACUM sessions, a proven job analysis method that identifies the key duties, tasks, and skills defining an occupation, guided by expert panels. The WIDS Course Design Workshop builds on those results to develop a complete, performance-based course, from learning outcomes to interactive, learner-centered activities.

  • Industry-Aligned Curriculum Design
  • Measurable Learning & Performance Outcomes
  • Benchmarking with International Standards and Review
